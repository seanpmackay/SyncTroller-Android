# SyncTroller Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-21_

SyncTroller does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The app communicates only with devices on your local network: the Philips
  Hue Play HDMI Sync Box and, optionally, a Hue Bridge. No data is sent to
  the developer or to any third-party server.
- Device access tokens issued by the Sync Box / Bridge during pairing are
  stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are used
  solely to talk to those devices. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
- Network permissions (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`,
  `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) are used only for
  local device discovery (mDNS) and local HTTP control. The notification /
  foreground-service permissions are used to show sync status while the box
  is syncing.
