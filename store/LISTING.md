## Full description (4000 chars max)
SyncTroller is a fast, standalone remote for the Philips Hue Play HDMI Sync Box.
It talks to the box directly over your home Wi-Fi — no cloud, no account, no
Signify login — so it opens instantly and responds instantly.

What you can do:
• Power the Sync Box on and off
• Switch between HDMI inputs
• Start and stop light sync
• Choose Video, Music or Game sync mode
• Set intensity (Subtle to Extreme) and brightness
• Pair with a Hue Bridge to see the entertainment area and bulb colour
• Keep a status notification in the shade to reopen the app in one tap

Setup is automatic: SyncTroller finds the Sync Box on your network via mDNS
and walks you through the one-time button-press pairing. If discovery doesn't
work on your network you can enter the box's IP address by hand.

Everything stays on your device. No analytics, no ads, no data collection —
see the privacy policy.
